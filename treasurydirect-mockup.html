Processing Times Notice

Heavy volume is slowing our response time to requests sent by mail. Some transactions require at least six weeks of processing time.

View full processing time details
  • Cashing paper savings bonds where you are named on the bonds: 6+ weeks
  • Unlocking your account or updating banking information: 6+ weeks
  • Converting paper bonds to electronic format: 6+ weeks
  • Lost, stolen, or missing savings bonds: 7+ months
  • Other requests including trusts: 10+ months
